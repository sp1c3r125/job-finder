About the data

{% if demo %}

This is a demo. Every job, company name, score, summary and evidence line on this site is invented for the demo. The links point to example.invalid, which does not exist. No real job board was read, no resume was read, and no AI model was called to make these pages.

The real tool runs privately on its owner's computer, reads public job feeds, scores jobs against a resume and drafts notes for a human to review. It never sends anything or applies anywhere.

{% else %}

Jobs come from public job-board APIs and feeds: Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby company boards, Himalayas, Remote OK and We Work Remotely. Scores and drafts are made by an AI model from the facts in your resume and must be read by you before anything is used. Nothing is sent or applied for you.
