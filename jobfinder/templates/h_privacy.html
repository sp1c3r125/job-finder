Privacy

Version {{ consent_version }}. This is an invite-only beta. We keep as little as we can and you can delete everything yourself.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • The facts we read from your resume, including its text, so we can match jobs and write what you ask for. The file you upload is not kept.
  • Your job matches and scores, and the cover notes and tailored resume text you ask for.
  • A record of your model calls: the day, the model and the number of tokens. This is how we keep daily limits and track cost.
  • When you last signed in.
  • Our logs record events with a scrambled user code. They never hold your resume or job text.

Who else handles it

  • Anthropic (the AI model provider) reads your resume facts and job posts to score matches and write drafts.
  • Supabase stores the database and runs the sign-in.
  • The hosting provider runs this service.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send anything to an employer. We never apply for you.
