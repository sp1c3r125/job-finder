How it works

Job sites ask you to guess the right job title. Your resume already says what you can do.

  1. 1

    We read your resume

    We copy out your roles, skills and dates. Anything we cannot find word for word in your resume is left out, and we show you what.

  2. 2

    We search where jobs are posted

    Company job boards and remote job sites, every day. We keep only roles that fit your titles and are open to the Philippines or APAC.

  3. 3

    You decide where to apply

    Each match shows why it fits and what is missing. Tailor your resume in one click, then apply on the original job page yourself.

Our rules

  • We never apply for you

    Nothing is sent to an employer. You read every draft and apply on the original job page.

  • We never add to your experience

    A tailored resume uses only your own lines. It does not add skills, numbers or job titles.

  • We credit every source

    Each match links to the original post and names the site it came from. Jobs past their expiry date are left out.
