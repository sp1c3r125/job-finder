Before you start

Please read this. You can delete your account and everything we hold about you at any time from the Account page.

  • What we keep: your email address, the facts we read from your resume (including its text), your job matches and scores, the cover notes you ask for, and a count of your daily model calls.
  • Who else sees your resume text: the AI model provider (Anthropic) reads it to find your matches and to write the drafts you ask for. Our database and hosting providers store it for us.
  • What we never do: send anything to an employer, apply for you, or add anything to your experience that is not in your resume.
  • Other people: they cannot see your data, and we do not use it for anything except giving you your matches.
